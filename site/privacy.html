Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-25

Fiscal is an app made by Rafael Escaleira (byescaleira). Your data belongs to you: it stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account. We do not run servers that receive it, and we never see it.

Data we collect

None. Fiscal has no account sign-up, no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs and no tracking across apps or websites.

Where your data lives

  • On your device. Your items and settings are stored locally.
  • In your iCloud. If iCloud is on, Fiscal syncs that data through your private iCloud database. Apple stores it under your Apple Account; we cannot access it.

Network requests the app makes

  • App settings. Fiscal reads feature settings from a public iCloud database. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions and in-app purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.
  • Fiscal on the Web (optional, Fiscal Pro). If you scan the QR code shown at fiscal.byescaleira.com/app, Fiscal sends a summary of your data, encrypted end to end for the browser whose QR code you scanned, through our bridge at fiscal.byescaleira.com. The bridge keeps it for at most 24 hours and cannot read it; only that browser can open it. Disconnect in Settings deletes it right away. The App Store label stays "Data Not Collected" because neither we nor any third party can access the content.

Diagnostics

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous usage and crash data through App Store Connect.

Deleting your data

You can manage storage for Fiscal in your device's iCloud settings. Deleting the app removes local data.

Children

Fiscal is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
