Questions

Before you download.

Do I need an account?

No. Fiscal works without signing up. Your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.

Where is my data stored?

On your devices and, with iCloud on, in your own private iCloud. There is no Fiscal account or server. Read the Privacy Policy.

What happens if I cancel Pro?

Everything you recorded stays readable and editable. Only the Pro features stop.

Which devices and languages?

iPhone and iPad today, with Mac and Apple Vision Pro coming soon. In English, Portuguese, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese and Russian.
