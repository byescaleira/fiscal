Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to us and include your device and Fiscal version (Settings › About Fiscal).

support@byescaleira.com

My data isn't syncing

Check that you are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on in every device, and that iCloud is on for Fiscal.

How do I change the language?

Fiscal follows the language of your device. To pick another one, open Settings › Language in Fiscal.

How do I cancel Fiscal Pro?

Open the subscriptions of your Apple Account in the Settings app and cancel Fiscal Pro there. Your data stays readable after the subscription ends.

I bought something but don't see it

Check that you are signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase, then open Fiscal again. Purchases are tied to that Apple Account.
